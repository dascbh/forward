# Sharpen FORWARD — diagnosis

## What already works (data after 2026-09-28, ADR-0018)

- Review rounds within budget: at most 5 findings and 0–1 blocking. Before, 6–20 findings and 1–5 blocking.
- FWD-022/023/024 each took 10–25 min.
- The split on the real diff fired (DEM-036, 988 lines).
- Specs are about 20× smaller.

## Loops still open, ranked by time lost

1. **Findings that do not block became mandatory cycle work.** The 0.17.0
   `## Cycle` requires "concluded means fixed, or declined by the user".
   This undoes fde-review's "anything else records".
   - Evidence: C-3 fixed all 10 findings plus the notes; in headlabs, C-2 e04045d became "the owner's decision: fix everything".
   - Change: only a blocking finding and the declared done items belong to the cycle. A non-blocking finding goes to the backlog by default.
2. **Owner decisions in the middle of a demand.** 41 commits and 201 mentions.
   DEM-025/026 waited about 19 h.
   - Causes: the spec's "Ask first" block (agents/fde-spec.md:30), the user's choice when a budget is spent, and every residual the 0.17.0 rule makes the user decline.
   - Change: one sign-off per demand, at spec approval, that collects every question. Otherwise take the stricter reading and record it without asking.
3. **Acceptance amended after construction.** 51 amendment commits: DEM-027 v1→v9 in 9 h, DEM-021 with 11.
   - Causes: the spec is written before the ADR, and the gate only checks `date:`.
   - Change: at M/L, the acceptance is committed together with the ADR. An amendment after the first behavior commit sends the demand back to triage.
4. **One decision lives in several places.** An ADR amendment plus an acceptance amendment for the same decision (8 pairs), 107 docs-only commits, and MNT-1 in 22 findings.
   - Change: the ADR is the only home. The acceptance cites it by id, and `architecture.md` goes away.
5. **Promotion became a hidden review round.** 15 of 18 promotions are conditional; mutation runs in 15; new findings surfaced at promotion (DEM-037 OBS-1, DEM-035 R1).
   - Change: promotion checks only the declared criteria, and a new defect goes to the backlog. Deploy gets one `deploy.md` per demand, written once after the first hour, with at most 5 conditions.
6. **LOC underestimated.** Specs estimate code only, but tests are 2–10× the code.
   - Change: count production lines only, in triage and in review. Split when the diff is above 2× the estimate and above 150 lines.
7. **The same observability finding in every demand.** OBS-1 appeared in 16 demands.
   - Change: every entry in `failure-modes.toml` carries a `signal` field.

## Inconsistencies (15), the ones that most mislead an agent

1. A review finding goes "to the backlog" (fde-review:31, fde-adversarial:68) but must be "concluded in the cycle" (AGENTS `## Cycle`).
2. XS has no spec, but review requires a threat model and criteria, so every XS review opens with a spurious finding.
3. M requires an ADR, but only architecture writes ADRs, and architecture enters at L only.
4. SETUP.md:108 and README:157 still say weight "decides what blocks merge".
5. L is "all six roles" (AGENTS) versus "all five" (fde-triage).
6. When the budget is spent, fde-review says "the builder records it in promotion"; AGENTS says "the user decides, `[-]` in the cycle".
7. Write scopes differ between roles.toml, AGENTS.md and agents/*.md (infra/**, walkthroughs/**).
8. Promotion reads `artifacts/gate-report.json`, which nothing produces.
9. The findings template is not installed in clients and lacks the `backlog` key.
10. ADR-0017 still describes the next-cycle list. No ADR records FWD-023.
11. Three diff ceilings (300 / 400 / 800).
12. invariants.toml cites `--gate adversarial-incentive`, which does not exist, and an `fde verify` CLI that does not exist.
13. fde-walkthrough says "never at XS/S" and "opt-in at every size".
14. The backlog line format differs between AGENTS and fde-scrum.
15. fde-spec requires an Always/Ask/Never block that nothing else mentions.

## Verbosity

- Loaded in every session: AGENTS.md is 2.4k words; skill and agent descriptions are 1.2k.
- Cuttable across the kernel: about 5k words of rationale, history and repetition that do not change behavior. That content belongs in ADRs.
- AGENTS.md: the RULE paragraph, step 3 (it duplicates survey and design), the graph/erosion Detail, and Scrum. About 750 of 2.4k words.
- Descriptions: at most 40 words each, trigger phrases only. Three skills showed no description in the listing, which suggests truncation.
- Agents: the body repeats the description, the handoff boilerplate appears in every file, and walkthrough-evaluator has about 550 cuttable words.
- Skills: RULE rationale in triage (~420), walkthrough (~1.5k), design (~260), erosion (~190), review (~200).
